| 14.6 | both | recognition_measurement | This paragraph specifies when and how dividend income from investments in associates shall be recognised, establishing the measurement principle for recognising distributions regardless of timing relative to acquisition. |
| 14.7 | both | other | This paragraph has been deleted and contains no substantive requirement. |

| 14.8A | PR2024 | recognition_measurement | This paragraph establishes when and how to test an interest in an associate for impairment, specifying the measurement approach and scope of the impairment test under Section 27. |
| 14.9 | both | recognition_measurement | This paragraph specifies the initial measurement method for investments in associates when using the optional accounting policy in paragraph 14.4(c). |
| 14.10 | pre-PR2024 | recognition_measurement | This paragraph specifies the measurement basis for investments in associates (fair value) and where changes in fair value are recognized (other comprehensive income or profit or loss), which are core recognition and measurement requirements. |
| 14.10 | PR2024 | recognition_measurement | This paragraph specifies the measurement basis (fair value) for investments in associates and prescribes how changes in fair value are recognised in the financial statements. |
| 14.10A | both | recognition_measurement | This paragraph establishes the recognition principle for when dividends and distributions from an associate should be recognised as income, regardless of their timing relative to acquisition. |
| 14.11 | both | other | A deleted paragraph contains no substantive requirement or guidance and is therefore classified as other. |
| 14.12 | both | disclosure | The paragraph explicitly requires specific information to be disclosed in the financial statements, particularly regarding related party transactions and balances. |
| 14.13 | pre-PR2024 | disclosure | The paragraph requires that investors disclose specific financial information (dividends and distributions recognised as income) in the notes or financial statements. |
| 14.13 | PR2024 | disclosure | The paragraph explicitly requires disclosure of the amount of dividends and other distributions recognised as income from investments in associates, which is information to be provided in the notes. |
| 14.14 | pre-PR2024 | disclosure | The paragraph requires an investor to separately disclose in the financial statements its share of profit/loss and discontinued operations of associates, which is a specific information presentation requirement. |
| 14.14 | PR2024 | disclosure | This paragraph requires an investor to provide specific information about its share of profit/loss and discontinued operations of associates in the financial statements or notes. |
| 14.15 | both | disclosure | The paragraph requires an investor to make specific disclosures about investments in associates by cross-referencing paragraphs 11.43 and 11.44, which are disclosure requirements. |
| 14.15A | both | disclosure | This paragraph requires disclosure of summarised financial information about investments in associates and the effect of equity method accounting in the notes to individual financial statements. |
