## Classification (para_type)

| Reference | Edition | para_type | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1AA.1 | pre-PR2024 | presentation | This paragraph governs the format and structure of how small entities must present their statement of financial position by requiring compliance with specific regulatory schedules. |
| 1AA.1 | PR2024 | presentation | This paragraph establishes which format and structure small entities must use when presenting their statement of financial position, governing the format requirements rather than recognition, measurement, or disclosure of specific items. |
| 1AA.2 | both | disclosure | This paragraph requires small entities to provide additional disclosures in the notes when drawing up an abridged balance sheet to ensure a true and fair view is achieved. |
| 1AA.3 | pre-PR2024 | presentation | This paragraph specifies the mandatory line items and structural requirements (distinguishing current from non-current) that must appear on the face of the statement of financial position. |
| 1AA.3 | PR2024 | presentation | This paragraph prescribes the mandatory line items and structure that must appear on the face of the statement of financial position, including the requirement to distinguish between current and non-current items. |
| 1AA.4 | pre-PR2024 | presentation | This paragraph prescribes the specific sub-classifications of line items that must be shown either on the face of the statement or in notes, governing the structure and breakdown of balance sheet items. |
| 1AA.4 | PR2024 | disclosure | The paragraph requires specific sub-classifications of balance sheet line items to be disclosed either on the statement of financial position or in the notes. |
| 1AA.5 | both | presentation | This paragraph governs the permitted flexibility in ordering, formatting and aggregation of balance sheet line items for small entities, which is a presentation matter. |
| 1AA.6 | both | presentation | This paragraph specifies how items must be classified and presented on the face of the statement of financial position (current vs non-current distinctions), which is a structural/ordering requirement. |
