## Classification (para_type)

| Reference | Edition | para_type | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | both | presentation | This paragraph establishes the structural requirement that entities must present total comprehensive income in one or two statements and governs the format of financial statements. |
| 5.1A | pre-PR2024 | scope_transition | This paragraph defines the scope exemption for small entities applying Section 1A, establishing which entities are not required to comply with the section. |
| 5.1A | PR2024 | scope_transition | This paragraph defines the scope of Section 5 by specifying which entities are exempt from its requirements and cross-references transitional guidance in Section 1A for small entities. |
| 5.2 | both | presentation | This paragraph specifies the format and structure for presenting total comprehensive income, requiring entities to choose between prescribed presentation methods for a financial statement. |
| 5.3 | both | scope_transition | This paragraph addresses transitional provisions governing changes between two different presentation approaches and how accounting policy change rules apply, which is a scope and transition matter rather than a substantive requirement itself. |
| 5.4 | both | other | Deleted paragraphs contain no substantive requirements or guidance. |
| 5.5 | both | presentation | This paragraph directly governs the format and structure of the statement of comprehensive income by specifying how items must be presented. |
| 5.5A | both | presentation | This paragraph specifies which line items shall be presented in the statement of comprehensive income, governing the structure and content of that financial statement. |
| 5.5B | both | presentation | This paragraph prescribes the mandatory line items that must be presented in the statement of comprehensive income when an entity adapts the profit and loss account format. |
| 5.5C | both | presentation | This paragraph governs the structure, line items, descriptions, and ordering of items in the income statement, which are presentation matters. |
| 5.6 | both | presentation | This paragraph specifies that particular items shall be presented in the statement of comprehensive income, governing the format, structure and placement of components within that financial statement. |
| 5.7 | both | presentation | This paragraph specifies the format and structure for presenting items in the income statement under the two-statement approach, governing which items must appear and how they should be organized. |
| 5.7A | both | presentation | This paragraph prescribes the specific line items and their ordering that must appear on the face of the income statement and statement of comprehensive income. |
| 5.7B | both | presentation | This paragraph governs where specific information must be presented (in the income statement versus elsewhere) and therefore concerns the format and placement of financial statement items. |
| 5.7C | both | presentation | This paragraph specifies the required format, structure, and ordering of line items within the statement of comprehensive income, governing how the statement shall be laid out rather than what must be disclosed in notes. |
| 5.7D | both | presentation | The paragraph specifies that turnover must be presented on the face of the income statement, which is a requirement about the format, structure, and placement of items on the financial statements. |
| 5.7E | both | presentation | This paragraph specifies where information must be presented (on the face of the income statement or statement of comprehensive income), which is a presentation requirement regarding statement placement and format. |
| 5.7F | both | disclosure | The paragraph requires an entity to re-present comparative period disclosures about discontinued operations, which is a requirement to provide information in the financial statements. |
| 5.8 | both | recognition_measurement | This paragraph establishes the fundamental principle for when and where income and expense items are recognised, requiring recognition in profit or loss except where FRS permits otherwise. |
| 5.9 | both | presentation | This paragraph governs the format and structure of the statement of comprehensive income by specifying when additional line items, headings and subtotals shall be presented on the face of the statement. |
| 5.9B | both | disclosure | The paragraph specifies what should and should not be included when disclosing operating profit, establishing requirements for the content and composition of information if an entity chooses to disclose it. |
| 5.10 | both | presentation | This paragraph requires entities to not present or describe items of income or expense as 'extraordinary items' in the statement of comprehensive income or notes, which governs how such items must be formatted and described. |
| 5.10A | both | other | This paragraph provides a definition of 'ordinary activities' that establishes the conceptual scope for the financial reporting framework, without imposing specific recognition, measurement, presentation, or disclosure requirements. |
| 5.10B | both | other | This paragraph provides definitions and clarifications of what constitutes extraordinary items without imposing new recognition, measurement, presentation, or disclosure requirements. |
| 5.11 | both | other | This paragraph contains no substantive requirement as it has been deleted from the standard. |
